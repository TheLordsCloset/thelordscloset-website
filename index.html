A little about us

Mission

Steward what we already have

The Lord’s Closet offers quality secondhand goods at fair prices, welcomes every neighbor, and exists to serve members of our local community and to raise funds for ministries that help those in need and share the Gospel.

Short history

Different locations, consistent vision

The ministry began as an extension of The Lord’s Cupboard. However, when the incoming donations exceeded the capacity of that ministry location, founder Pat Gwinn, with the help of her husband Buddy, established The Lord’s Closet in a separate building. The location has changed over the years, but the vision has stayed the same - helping our neighbors and the broader community.

Where the profits go

Serving others

After operating costs, proceeds help with local benevolence—clothing, household needs, and support for families in hard seasons. Proceeds also support ministries that help those in need and share the Gospel.
